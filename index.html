Make your YouTube, yours.

Everyone uses YouTube differently. Some people use it for technology, fitness, finance, gaming, creativity, education, entertainment, hobbies, news, or a mixture of everything. MakeMyTube is built around a simple idea: instead of letting one recommendation system decide everything for you, you should have more control over what your own feed becomes.

Your YouTube. Your rules.

MakeMyTube adds controls that help you shape recommendations, organize useful content, reduce distractions, and personalize the way you use YouTube.

You stay in control.

MakeMyTube does not decide what should be productive or useful for you. You choose the interests, topics, channels, and controls that fit the way you want to use YouTube.

1. Choose your interests

Add the subjects, categories, or interests that matter to you.

2. Reduce unwanted topics

Add blocked tags for content you personally do not want to appear as strongly in your experience.

3. Let your feed adapt

MakeMyTube works with the content YouTube already recommends and helps filter and organize it around your preferences.

Subscription & payment information

MakeMyTube Pro costs ₹20 per month . The subscription renews monthly until cancellation is requested.

Users must sign in with Google before purchasing MakeMyTube Pro so the subscription can be associated with their account.

Payments are securely processed through Razorpay . MakeMyTube does not directly collect or store card numbers, CVV values, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Cancellation: Users can stop automatic renewal. When cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing cycle, Pro access remains available until the end of the already-paid period, and the subscription will not renew again.

Google Sign-In

MakeMyTube uses Google Sign-In to identify users and associate MakeMyTube Pro access with the correct account.

MakeMyTube requests basic Google identity information needed for account access, such as name, email address, account identifier, and profile information when available.


MakeMyTube does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or Google Contacts.

Privacy

MakeMyTube is designed to provide personalization and productivity features while keeping its requested permissions limited to what the extension needs to operate.
