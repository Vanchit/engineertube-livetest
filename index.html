Subscription and payment information

EngineerTube Pro costs ₹20 per month. The subscription renews monthly until cancellation is requested.

Users must sign in with Google before purchasing EngineerTube Pro so the subscription can be associated with their EngineerTube account.

Payments are securely processed through Razorpay. EngineerTube does not directly collect or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Cancellation: users may stop automatic renewal. When cancellation is scheduled at the end of the current billing cycle, Pro access remains available until the end of the already-paid billing period, and the subscription will not renew again.

Google Sign-In

EngineerTube uses Google Sign-In to identify users and associate their EngineerTube Pro subscription with their account.

EngineerTube requests only basic Google identity information, including name, email address, account identifier, and profile information when available.

EngineerTube does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or Google Contacts.
