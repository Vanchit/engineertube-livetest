MakeMyTube Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the MakeMyTube Chrome Extension.

MakeMyTube is a Chrome extension that gives users more control over their YouTube experience.

MakeMyTube can help shape recommendations, reduce unwanted topics, prioritize selected creators, save videos, create notes, track activity, and provide optional productivity and distraction-control features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information MakeMyTube accesses, how that information is used, where it may be stored, which third-party services are involved, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Information We Access and Process

MakeMyTube accesses and processes information only when needed to provide extension functionality, authentication, personalization, productivity tools, and subscription services.

Google Account Information

When you choose to sign in with Google, MakeMyTube may receive:

  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google profile picture, when available.

MakeMyTube requests only the Google identity scopes: openid, email, and profile.

This information is used to authenticate you, display your account, and associate your account with MakeMyTube Pro access where applicable.

YouTube Page and Activity Information

MakeMyTube processes information available on YouTube pages so that recommendation filtering, saved-video tools, notes, trusted channels, progress tracking, and interface controls can operate.

Depending on the features you use, this information may include:

  • Video titles.
  • Video identifiers.
  • Video URLs.
  • Channel names.
  • Video duration.
  • Playback progress.
  • Watch time.
  • Focus Mode activity.
  • Normal Mode activity.
  • Saved videos.
  • Timestamped notes.
  • Selected tags.
  • Blocked tags.
  • Trusted-channel preferences.
  • Pomodoro session information.
  • Progress information.
  • Productivity statistics.

2. Information Stored in Browser Storage

A significant portion of MakeMyTube data is stored using Chrome extension storage.

This may include:

  • MakeMyTube settings.
  • Recommendation preferences.
  • Selected tags.
  • Blocked tags.
  • Trusted channels.
  • Saved videos.
  • Timestamped notes.
  • Pomodoro information.
  • Watch-time statistics.
  • Progress information.
  • Subscription status cached for the interface.

This information allows MakeMyTube to remember your preferences across browser sessions.

3. Local Import and Export

MakeMyTube may allow users to manually export supported video, progress, notes, or related extension information to a local JSON file.

Compatible backup files can later be imported to restore supported information after reinstalling the extension or moving to another supported browser environment.

MakeMyTube does not automatically upload exported backup files to a cloud storage service.

Users are responsible for protecting backup files stored on their devices.

4. MakeMyTube Backend

MakeMyTube uses backend infrastructure hosted using Cloudflare Workers and related Cloudflare services.

Backend information may include:

  • Google account identifier.
  • Email address.
  • Name.
  • Profile picture URL when available.
  • MakeMyTube subscription plan.
  • Subscription status.
  • Subscription start information.
  • Subscription expiry information.
  • Razorpay subscription identifiers.
  • Payment or subscription event identifiers.

This information is used for authentication and subscription management.

5. Payments and Razorpay

MakeMyTube Pro payments are processed using Razorpay.

Users may be redirected to Razorpay's payment interface when purchasing MakeMyTube Pro.

MakeMyTube does not directly collect or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or similar sensitive payment credentials.

Razorpay processes payment information according to its own privacy and security practices.

MakeMyTube receives only information needed to manage subscription status and billing periods.

6. Google Sign-In

MakeMyTube uses Google OAuth for authentication.

Google Sign-In is used to:

  • Verify your identity.
  • Create or identify your MakeMyTube account.
  • Display basic account information.
  • Associate your account with MakeMyTube Pro access.

MakeMyTube does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or unrelated Google account content.

7. Chrome Extension Permissions

Storage

Used to store extension preferences, selected tags, saved videos, notes, progress information, Pomodoro data, and account state.

Identity

Used for Google authentication.

Alarms

Used for Pomodoro timers and related timed functionality.

Declarative Net Request

Used by MakeMyTube's optional advertisement-reduction functionality through packaged browser rules.

YouTube Host Access

Used to provide recommendation filtering, saved-video tools, interface controls, notes, progress tracking, trusted-channel functionality, and other MakeMyTube features.

Backend Access

Used for authentication and MakeMyTube Pro subscription management.

8. How We Use Information

  • Authenticating users.
  • Providing recommendation modes.
  • Filtering content according to user preferences.
  • Applying blocked tags.
  • Prioritizing trusted channels.
  • Saving user settings.
  • Providing saved-video tools.
  • Providing timestamped notes.
  • Tracking supported activity and progress.
  • Providing Pomodoro functionality.
  • Managing MakeMyTube Pro subscriptions.
  • Maintaining extension reliability.

9. Data Sharing

MakeMyTube does not sell user data.

MakeMyTube does not share user information for advertising profiling, lending, or unrelated purposes.

Information may be processed by:

  • Google — for Google OAuth authentication.
  • Cloudflare — for backend infrastructure and security.
  • Razorpay — for payment and subscription processing.

10. Advertising

MakeMyTube does not use personal information to build advertising profiles.

MakeMyTube may provide optional features designed to reduce certain advertising-related elements or requests on YouTube.

These features do not involve selling or sharing personal information with advertisers.

11. Remote Code

MakeMyTube does not download and execute remotely hosted JavaScript as extension application logic.

Extension logic is packaged with the Chrome extension.

12. Data Retention

Information stored in Chrome extension storage may remain until it is cleared, reset, overwritten, removed through MakeMyTube controls, or removed according to Chrome's extension-storage behavior.

Account and subscription information may be retained as reasonably necessary to:

  • Maintain account access.
  • Provide subscription access.
  • Process cancellations.
  • Process subscription expiration.
  • Prevent duplicate payment processing.
  • Meet operational or legal requirements.

13. User Choices and Data Deletion

Users can remove many types of locally stored MakeMyTube information through extension controls.

Users may also uninstall MakeMyTube at any time.

14. Security

MakeMyTube uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the service.

  • HTTPS communication.
  • Google OAuth authentication.
  • Server-side payment credentials.
  • Razorpay webhook verification.
  • Restricted extension host permissions.
  • Server-side subscription verification.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

15. Children's Privacy

MakeMyTube is a general-purpose browser customization and productivity extension.

It is not specifically directed toward children below the minimum age required to independently use Google accounts, payment services, or Chrome services in their jurisdiction.

16. Third-Party Services

MakeMyTube interacts with YouTube, Google, Cloudflare, and Razorpay.

Use of these services may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

MakeMyTube is an independent Chrome extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
